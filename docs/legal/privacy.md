## The short version

- You can use Thema without an account. Then your project stays in your own browser.
- If you sign in with GitHub or Google, we get your name, email address and profile picture link from them. We never see or store a password.
- We keep your saved projects so you can open them on any device.
- We don't sell your data, don't show ads and don't use tracking cookies.

## What we collect, and why

**Without an account**
- **Your project, in your browser.** The sites you add, your settings and your blended system are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device until you clear them.
- **The website addresses you paste.** To read a site's design, our server opens it in a browser and records its visible styles (colors, fonts, spacing) and a screenshot of the page. We keep the result for 24 hours so the same site loads instantly again that day.
- **A scrambled form of your IP address,** to apply the daily limit on new extractions. We keep it only for the current day. Your IP address itself isn't stored.

**With an account** (also everything above)
- **From GitHub or Google:** your name, email address and the link to your profile picture. Thema shows your initials, not the picture.
- **Your session:** when you sign in, we record the IP address and browser of that sign-in, to keep you signed in and to protect your account.
- **Your saved projects:** the site addresses, your settings, the extracted systems and your blend.
- **Screenshots** of the sites in your saved projects, so their cards still show a picture after 24 hours.
- **Your plan** (Free or Pro).

**Payments** (once Pro is on sale): payments are handled by Paddle (see below). We receive whether you have an active Pro subscription and basic order details, never your card number.

## Cookies and local storage

- **One sign-in cookie,** only if you sign in. It keeps you signed in and is removed when you sign out.
- **Local storage** for your project (described above).
- **No advertising cookies, tracking or analytics.**

## How long we keep things

- Extraction results and screenshots that aren't in a saved project: 24 hours.
- The scrambled IP address for daily limits: until the end of the day (UTC).
- Sessions: until you sign out or the session expires.
- Saved projects: until you delete them. A saved project's screenshots are deleted with the last project that uses them.
- Your account: until you delete it (account menu → Delete account), which removes it right away, with your sessions, saved projects and their screenshots.

## Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (and, if you're in the EU or UK, the GDPR), you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, get a copy, object to how we use it, or have it deleted. You can delete saved projects yourself on the Projects screen, and your whole account from the account menu (top right) → Delete account: it's removed right away, with everything saved in it. For anything else, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account and we'll answer within [30] days. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) or your local data protection authority.
